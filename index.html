Why ISAT

ISAT (Integrated Science and Technology) is JMU's ABET-accredited, hands-on B.S. program for solving real-world problems across disciplines instead of specializing narrowly from day one. It let me explore before committing to a concentration in Manufacturing and Applied Computing junior year. At its core, ISAT has been an engineering and manufacturing education for me: designing in CAD, building and testing real hardware, and learning how things are actually made, from the machines on the shop floor to the systems that run them. It has also given me a strong base in project management, which keeps that kind of hands-on work organized and on schedule.
