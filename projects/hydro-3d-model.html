Water: forebay at ≈1,154 ft, river at ≈1,114 ft
Recommended vortex layout: 5 conical basins in series on switchback terraces beside the cascade. Every basin carries the full 21 cfs and drops 6.4 ft (1.95 m), inside the 0.7–3 m range vortex turbines work in. Level flumes along the contour join the basins, each entering tangentially. The last outlet sits at ≈1,117 ft, above the river. Expected output ≈ 26 kW (20–31 kW at 35–55% water-to-wire), ≈43 kW at the 23 MGD design flow, ≈213 MWh/yr. Generators are 5 × 10 kW.
Basins sit on cut-and-fill pads with the floor at the pad, and flumes run on piers, so everything is above grade and out of the river.
Penstock concept: 30 in HDPE penstock, ≈38.7 ft net head, turbines in parallel, ≈52 kW at 21 cfs.
Included: forebay tank, trash rack, overflow weir, isolation, air and surge valves, anchor blocks, manifold, turbine-generators with draft tubes, control room, transformer, fence, tailrace aeration, plant tie-in.
Scale comes from your 329 ft ruler measurement (≈0.51 ft per image pixel). Elevations are estimates from USGS 3DEP LiDAR spot heights (plant ≈1,149–1,161 ft, river ≈1,114 ft). The slope between them is smoothed, not surveyed. Basin sizes, positions and all new structures are illustrative examples, not designs. The penstock is drawn above grade so you can see it; it would be buried.
